Welcome to IOC Triage

IOC Triage blocks phishing sites, warns on phishing emails in Gmail and Outlook, stops your password being typed on fake login pages, and gives SOC analysts instant verdicts.

Turn on browsing protection

To protect you, IOC Triage has to read some of your data. Please read this first. Protection stays off until you agree.

Read and kept on this device

  • The address of each page you open, to check it for phishing
  • The text of pages with a password field, to spot fake logins
  • Emails you open in Gmail and Outlook on the web, to spot phishing
  • Your work password when you sign in on a work login page. Only a salted hash is stored, and it is compared with what you type in password fields

Can leave this device

  • For an already suspicious site, its domain name goes to rdap.org to check the domain's age
  • Nothing else, unless you or your admin connect a console

No analytics, no tracking, no ads and no sale of data.

You can withdraw this any time in Settings. Privacy policy

Browsing protection stays off. The analyst panel below still works, and you can turn protection on from the toolbar button.

Browsing protection is on

You can turn it off any time in Settings.

Private by design

Phishing checks run on this device. When you investigate an indicator, only that indicator goes to the intel services. Nothing else is sent unless you connect your own AI or a company console.

Work in a SOC? Meet the analyst panel

  • Select any IP, domain, URL, hash or CVE, right-click, IOC Triage: Investigate: one verdict from 8 free intel sources
  • One-click ticket notes, email header analysis, safe link detonation, and client memory
  • Add free VirusTotal and AbuseIPDB keys (2 minutes) for known-bad verdicts

Free IOC Triage community protection

Connect to the IOC Triage community service to receive a list of new phishing sites (updated four times a day). You can also share what IOC Triage blocks, so everyone in the community is protected sooner.

Sent when you share reports

  • Address of a site IOC Triage blocked or warned about, or that you reported, bypassed or trusted
  • For a phishing email: sender, subject and the suspicious links
  • Your public IP address, browser type and IOC Triage version
  • The nickname below, if you give one

Never sent

  • Your passwords (only a salted hash stays on this device)
  • Email bodies or attachments
  • Your browsing history: normal sites are never reported
  • Anything at all when you choose "Protect only"

Reports are kept for up to 30 days and used only to improve phishing protection. You can change your choice any time from the IOC Triage toolbar button.

"Protect only" still downloads the phishing list, so the console sees your IP address when it does.
