Welcome to SOCLens

SOCLens is already protecting this browser: it blocks phishing sites, warns on phishing emails in Gmail and Outlook, and stops your password being typed on fake login pages.

Private by design

Every check runs on this device. SOCLens sends nothing to any server unless you, or your company's admin, connect it to a SOCLens console.

Work in a SOC? Meet the analyst panel

  • Select any IP, domain, URL, hash or CVE, right-click, SOCLens: Investigate: one verdict from 8 free intel sources
  • One-click ticket notes, email header analysis, safe link detonation, and client memory
  • Add free VirusTotal and AbuseIPDB keys (2 minutes) for known-bad verdicts

Free SOCLens community protection

Connect to the SOCLens community service to receive a list of new phishing sites (updated four times a day). You can also share what SOCLens blocks, so everyone in the community is protected sooner.

Sent when you share reports

  • Address of a site SOCLens blocked or warned about, or that you reported, bypassed or trusted
  • For a phishing email: sender, subject and the suspicious links
  • Your public IP address, browser type and SOCLens version
  • The nickname below, if you give one

Never sent

  • Your passwords (only a salted hash stays on this device)
  • Email bodies or attachments
  • Your browsing history: normal sites are never reported
  • Anything at all when you choose "Protect only"

Reports are kept for up to 30 days and used only to improve phishing protection. You can change your choice any time from the SOCLens toolbar button.

"Protect only" still downloads the phishing list, so the console sees your IP address when it does.
